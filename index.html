Our Services

Our Professional Services

At Precision Auto Care, we offer professional repair and maintenance solutions for all vehicle types. Our experienced technicians use modern equipment and industry-approved techniques to ensure quality, safety and customer satisfaction.

Engine Repairs

Our technicians perform detailed engine diagnostics to identify faults and repair engine components efficiently. Regular engine maintenance improves vehicle performance, fuel efficiency and reliability.

Vehicle Servicing

Routine vehicle servicing includes oil changes, fluid checks, filter replacement and safety inspections. Scheduled servicing helps prevent unexpected breakdowns and extends the life of your vehicle.

Brake Repairs

We repair and replace brake pads, discs and other braking components. Proper brake maintenance ensures vehicle safety and reliable stopping performance.

Wheel Alignment

Wheel alignment improves steering control, tyre lifespan and fuel economy. Our workshop uses specialised equipment to ensure accurate wheel positioning.

Battery Replacement

We provide battery testing, maintenance and replacement services. A healthy battery ensures reliable vehicle starting and electrical performance.

Vehicle Inspections

Comprehensive inspections identify potential mechanical issues before they become serious problems. Regular inspections improve vehicle safety and reliability.
