Our Services

At Precision Auto Care, we offer a wide range of vehicle repair and maintenance services designed to keep your vehicle safe, reliable, and performing at its best. Our skilled technicians use modern equipment and industry-approved techniques to provide quality service.

Engine Diagnostics and Repairs

Our technicians use advanced diagnostic tools to identify engine problems quickly and accurately. We repair engine faults, improve performance and help prevent costly future breakdowns.

Vehicle Servicing

Regular vehicle servicing improves fuel efficiency, reliability and safety. Our service packages include oil changes, filter replacements and complete vehicle inspections.

Brake Repairs

We inspect, repair and replace brake components to ensure maximum safety. Our services include brake pads, brake discs and complete brake system maintenance.

Wheel Alignment

Proper wheel alignment improves tyre life, steering control and fuel economy. We use precision equipment to ensure your wheels are correctly aligned.

Battery Testing and Replacement

We test your battery performance and provide reliable battery replacements when required, ensuring dependable vehicle starts.

Vehicle Inspection

Comprehensive inspections help identify potential issues before they become major problems. Our inspections focus on safety, performance and reliability.
